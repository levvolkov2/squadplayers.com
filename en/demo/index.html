SQUAD
in action

A screen recording of a real iPhone: the TestFlight build is connected to the SQUAD production server. We show the player’s path — from the home screen to a match where everyone pays only their own share.

  1. Home“What are we playing today?”: player level ~2.5, sport switch, upcoming games, free courts and tournaments.
  2. PlayMatches, courts and tournaments with filters: my level, date, evening, district.
  3. New matchClub, date and free court time with the price per player.
  4. Format and levelDoubles 2×2, calibration or rated match, level range, open or private match.
  5. Pay your share“Everyone pays for their own spot”: the total with court cost and the SQUAD service fee — before card payment.
  6. ProfileProfile level, calibration 0 of 5, match stats, feed and players.
  7. City ratingThe Tashkent rating, based on confirmed results after five calibration matches.
  8. ChatsMatch chats and SQUAD support right in the app.

Important. Before the public launch the database contains only a test club and courts labelled “[REVIEW / TEST]”. No match is created and no payment is made in the video. Real clubs, starting with Itek Tennis Club, will appear by the launch in Q4 2026.
