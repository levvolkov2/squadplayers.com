The essentials

Any
questions?

When will the app be on the App Store and Google Play?
iOS 1.0.7 is already in TestFlight. The public App Store and Google Play launch is in Q4 2026, starting in Tashkent.
Which sports are available?
Padel and tennis. Football joins in 2027.
How do I sign in?
With your phone number and an SMS code. No password needed.
How do payments work?
By card in the app, 100% prepayment. The court cost is split between players — or one pays for all. The final price is shown before you pay.
When can I cancel a booking?
Free cancellation up to 12 hours before the start.
What does the 0.0–7.0 level mean?
It’s the player level scale. Your first five matches calibrate it; after every game you enter the score and rate each other.
How do I connect a club or become a coach?
Email ceo@squadplayers.com. Coaches apply in the app and work through SQUAD partner clubs.
